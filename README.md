# FIFA 2018 Player Analysis – Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes FIFA 2018 player data using Microsoft Power BI. The goal of the project was to explore player demographics, ratings, wages, positions, potential, nationalities, and club distributions while creating clear and informative data visualizations.

The project demonstrates my ability to clean and transform data using Power Query, build visualizations in Power BI, and interpret data to communicate meaningful insights.

## Dataset

The dataset contains information about professional football players included in FIFA 2018.

Some of the main variables used in the analysis include:

- Player name
- Age
- Nationality
- Club
- Preferred position
- Overall rating
- Potential rating
- Wage
- Market value
- Physical attributes
- Technical attributes

## Data Cleaning and Preparation

Before beginning the analysis, the dataset was cleaned and transformed using Power Query in Power BI.

The main data preparation steps included:

- Removed unnecessary columns that were not relevant to the analysis, such as player photos and club logos
- Removed currency symbols from wage and market value columns
- Converted values containing `K` and `M` into numerical values
- Converted columns into the appropriate data types
- Reviewed the dataset for missing or inconsistent values
- Prepared categorical and numerical variables for analysis
- Created cleaned versions of columns when necessary for visualization and comparison

These steps ensured that the data could be accurately analyzed and visualized within Power BI.

## Analysis

The Power BI report was created to answer a series of questions about FIFA 2018 players.

The analysis focused on areas such as:

- Countries with the largest number of players
- The relationship between player age and overall rating
- The age at which player development begins to level off
- Wage differences among offensive positions
- Top players based on overall rating and potential
- Player nationality distribution across clubs
- Identification of players with strong future potential
- Comparison of player characteristics across clubs and positions

## Key Insights

Several patterns were identified through the analysis.

### Player Nationality

Certain countries contribute significantly more professional players to the FIFA 2018 dataset than others. Comparing the number of players by nationality makes it possible to identify countries with the largest representation in professional football.

### Age and Player Rating

Player overall ratings generally increase during the earlier stages of a player's career before beginning to level off as players become older.

The relationship between age and overall rating helps illustrate how player performance and development change throughout a professional football career.

### Player Wages

Wages vary considerably among offensive positions and individual players.

Higher-rated players generally tend to earn higher wages, although wages can also vary depending on factors such as position, club, and player reputation.

### Overall Rating and Potential

Comparing overall rating with potential helps distinguish between players who are already performing at a high level and younger players who may have significant room for future development.

Players with relatively high potential compared with their current overall rating may represent promising future players.

### Club and Nationality Distribution

The analysis also shows how players from certain nationalities are distributed across different clubs.

Some clubs have a noticeably larger concentration of players from particular countries than others.
